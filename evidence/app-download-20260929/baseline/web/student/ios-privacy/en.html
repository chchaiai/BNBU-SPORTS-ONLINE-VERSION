BNBU Sports iOS Student Privacy Policy

Version: iOS-2026-09-24

Updated: 24 September 2026

This policy applies to the BNBU Sports iOS student app displaying this version. The BNBU Sports student development team ("we") operates this campus testing project for invited students at Beijing Normal-Hong Kong Baptist University (BNBU). We do not represent this project as an officially operated university service. Teachers and administrators continue to use the existing website; this policy describes the processing associated with students using shared services through the iOS app.

Privacy contact: HazeFarewe11@outlook.com, handled by Runji Zhou. Please read this policy before signing in or submitting information. You may contact us with questions before using the service.

1. Accounts, courses and student information

For identity verification, course association and profile display, we process relevant information such as your university email, student number, name, faculty, major, class, entry year, grade level, gender, date of birth, place of origin, student status and course relationships. Information comes from details you provide or confirm and teaching records maintained by authorised personnel.

Ordinary students sign in with university email verification codes. We process codes, validity periods and session credentials for authentication. Only explicitly authorised synthetic test accounts may use expiring, revocable passwords; this option is not for ordinary students. The app stores login tokens in iOS Keychain and necessary account and interface state on the device. Signing out does not delete an account.

2. Exercise records, applications and feedback

When you use the relevant features, we process activity types, start and end times, duration, pause status, descriptions, photos, videos, files, courses, credits, grades and review outcomes to support submissions, teaching review, queries and disputes. Authorised teachers or administrators can view relevant materials under their business permissions. Materials are not displayed as public social posts.

Exemption, certification or other applications may contain health, medical, disability or other sensitive information you choose to submit. Exercise and duration records may also reflect fitness activity. Submit only what the relevant process requires and avoid information about unrelated people. Feedback, attachments and replies are used to address your questions. Declining to provide a particular document may prevent the corresponding application from being completed; it does not constitute consent to unrelated processing.

3. Camera, microphone, photos and files

The app requests camera permission when you choose to capture evidence, application documents or course QR codes. Microphone permission is requested for video with sound. On-site capture uses the iOS native camera experience. When selecting photos or files, we process only materials you expressly select through the system picker, without scanning your entire photo library.

The app requests permission to add photos when you choose to save an original exercise photo to your library. Originals retained inside the app are separate from copies you export to Photos, Files or other locations.

Materials may be compressed, converted and checked for integrity before or after upload to meet evidence requirements and reduce unnecessary metadata. Do not upload unrelated private content. The app does not request foreground or background location permission, collect coordinates through location sensors or create movement tracks. Location metadata in uploads is not used as exercise evidence.

You can manage camera, microphone and photo permissions in iOS Settings. Refusing or withdrawing a permission affects the corresponding feature. You can still enter an invitation code manually if camera permission for scanning is declined.

4. Notifications and Live Activities

Following iOS notification authorisation, the app may associate an APNs device token, app version and language with your account for service notifications. Apple's push service processes the device token and notification content. You can disable notifications in Settings and still view available service information in the app. Signing out or deleting an account clears the corresponding push association.

Exercise Live Activities can display an activity name, time and status on the Lock Screen or supported Dynamic Island. Others who can see your screen may see this information. The app clears the corresponding Live Activity when you submit or discard a record or sign out. Manage Lock Screen visibility according to your preferences.

5. Connections, security logs and diagnostics

To provide the service, maintain security and investigate failures, requests may include device or system version, language, IP address, interface category, status and error codes, request identifiers and timestamps. Some failed requests cause the client to submit structured, field-limited diagnostics to the backend, potentially associated with the current account.

Native transport diagnostics record only failure stages, route categories, error-domain categories and numeric error codes in device system logs; this feature does not upload them to our server. iOS manages these local system logs. Ordinary logs should not contain passwords, verification codes, complete login tokens, complete signed media URLs or uploaded content.

The app has no advertising SDK, does not use advertising identifiers or cross-app advertising tracking, and does not sell personal information. It does not integrate HealthKit; exercise or health information you submit can nevertheless be personal information.

6. Providers and processing locations

Our core backend, database and media storage use Tencent Cloud services in Hong Kong. The app connects over HTTPS and uses time-limited authorised URLs for uploading or accessing photos, videos and attachments. Authorised maintainers should access information only as necessary for operations, support or security.

The primary verification-email provider is AOKSend, supplied by Shenzhen Qianke Network Technology Co., Ltd. It receives the recipient email address, verification code and validity period needed for delivery. The current Professional plan displays a 365-day detailed-statistics window. This is not a uniform deletion deadline for message bodies, delivery details and backups; the website's seven-day statement does not establish that all message copies are deleted after seven days. We are clarifying this provider's category-specific periods, processing locations and per-user deletion process. Contact our privacy mailbox for the latest information or assistance with a deletion request.

If the primary email channel fails, the backend may use Tencent Cloud SES as a fallback, currently configured for Guangzhou. Apple provides APNs and TestFlight, whose processing may involve other countries or regions. We therefore do not represent that all data is processed solely in Hong Kong or mainland China.

When you use TestFlight, Apple automatically collects usage information and crash logs and shares them with developers; this collection cannot be separately disabled within TestFlight. Feedback and screenshots you choose to submit are also shared with developers and may be associated with your name, email, device and system information. We use these materials only to improve the app and related products and do not pass them to other third parties. Avoid including unrelated personal information in feedback screenshots. Apple TestFlight privacy information: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/data/en/test-flight/ . AOKSend privacy information: https://www.aoksend.com/privacy.html . Provider notices do not replace our responsibility for our own processing.

We provide information to authorised personnel and providers only as necessary for the services, security, support and applicable legal obligations described here. Using the app does not authorise others to use your information for unrelated advertising or marketing. Legally required disclosures are handled under applicable requirements.

7. Retention and safeguards

Account information and related teaching, exercise, application and feedback records are retained while the account exists and they are needed for the corresponding service. Successful account deletion is handled as described below. Re-registration does not restore the old account's history.

Unsubmitted drafts are stored locally in the app and the corresponding drafts are cleared after successful submission or intentional discard. Separately retained original photos inside the app are user-manageable and cleared upon account deletion. You manage copies exported to Photos, Files or other recipients yourself.

The cloud database currently retains automatic data and log backups for seven days each. Manually created operational backups are outside this automatic seven-day rotation and currently have no uniform automatic expiry. Backups are for recovery and operations, not a feature for reopening deleted accounts. For deletion requests involving backups, we assess the executable scope and explain the outcome and limitations.

Necessary security audits and server diagnostics currently have no uniform fixed-day automatic purge. We do not promise automatic deletion after 30 or 180 days. Their use is limited to security, troubleshooting, disputes and applicable legal obligations. Contact us to request access or deletion; where retention is necessary for applicable legal or security purposes, we will explain the reasons and limitations. Email-provider retention is described above and is not covered by the cloud database's seven-day backup period.

We use measures such as encryption in transit, access controls, credential protection and audit logging. No storage or transmission method is absolutely secure. We will handle incidents requiring notification according to applicable requirements.

8. Account deletion and its scope

You can permanently delete your account in Profile → Settings → Delete account after verifying your current email code. Dedicated synthetic test accounts verify their password again instead.

Deletion removes your profile, course relationships, exercise records, credits and review history, along with account-associated applications, feedback and other business information, and revokes sessions and push associations. Server photos, videos and attachments enter an asynchronous deletion queue. During normal operation they are removed as soon as practicable and checked again after existing upload credentials expire. Failed jobs are retried. We cannot promise instantaneous erasure of all storage copies.

The app also clears the account's local drafts, internally retained original photos, caches and Live Activities. Incomplete local cleanup is retried on a later launch. The app does not automatically delete your Photos library, exported or shared files, or messages already received in a recipient's mailbox. Backups, necessary audits and email-provider data are subject to the preceding explanations.

Deletion is irreversible; check which materials you need to keep beforehand. Signing out, uninstalling the app or withdrawing system permissions does not replace server-side account deletion.

9. Other rights and contact

Email HazeFarewe11@outlook.com to request access, correction, deletion or an explanation of your personal information, withdraw consent-based processing, or seek help with unavailable email access, your account or submitted materials. Describe your request. We will ask only for information necessary to verify identity and address it, not passwords, verification codes or complete login tokens.

We handle requests under applicable requirements and explain the outcome. If applicable legal obligations, others' rights or necessary security restrictions prevent part of a request, we will explain why. Withdrawal of consent does not affect prior lawful processing, but may prevent continued use of functions depending on that information.

10. Minors and policy updates

The first external campus test cohort is limited to invited students aged 14 or older. Before students aged 14–17 participate, both the student and their guardian must read this policy and confirm participation; the project lead implements this arrangement before issuing invitations. Children under 14 are not invited to the first cohort. Contact us if you or your guardian need to leave the test or request action concerning personal information.

We will update this policy when services or processing change and explain material changes through the app's policy page and appropriate notices. Where renewed consent is required, we will handle it under applicable requirements. The Chinese and English versions describe the same scope; contact us to clarify any discrepancy.
